# TELAIO — Product Brief

## Positioning
**A motorsport CAD workstation and interactive 3D mechanical assembly simulator.**

TELAIO is a desktop-first browser application for exploring a detailed Formula 1-style vehicle as an engineering assembly. Users can inspect components, understand how systems connect, explode assemblies, remove parts, rebuild mechanisms and run transparent engineering studies.

## Reference vehicle
Initial visual reference: Ferrari SF-24, car number 16 (Charles Leclerc). This is a study reference only. TELAIO is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ferrari, Scuderia Ferrari or Formula 1. Use original or properly licensed geometry and imagery; do not imply access to proprietary CAD or confidential specifications.

## Product pillars
1. **CAD workstation:** precise viewport, assembly tree, selection tools, measurement and section/isolation controls.
2. **Interactive assembly simulator:** exploded views, dependency-aware disassembly, guided placement, constraints, snapping and validation.
3. **Engineering workbench:** component properties, coordinate frames, mass properties, braking, suspension and aerodynamic studies with visible assumptions and provenance.

## Primary users
Engineering students, educators, motorsport enthusiasts and learners who want to understand vehicle construction and engineering trade-offs.

## Core journey
Open vehicle → navigate assembly tree → hover/select component → inspect properties and interfaces → isolate or section → explode assembly → disassemble → rebuild and validate → run an engineering study.

## Quality bar
The product must feel like professional motorsport engineering software: precise, restrained, technically credible and visually deliberate. The 3D vehicle is the primary subject. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative telemetry, fake data, gratuitous gradients, excessive rounded cards and meaningless animation.

## Scope boundaries
This is an educational engineering environment, not a certified CAD package, race simulator, production design tool or validated CFD/FEA solver. Every engineering result must expose model assumptions, units, inputs, limitations and data provenance.
